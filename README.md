# 🌱 GreenGrid AI

### AI-Powered Smart Renewable Energy Management System

**GreenGrid AI** is a software-first intelligent energy management platform designed to maximize renewable-energy utilization in smart campuses.

It uses **AI-based prediction and optimization** to understand solar generation, building-wise energy demand, battery status, weather, occupancy patterns, and electricity tariffs. The system predicts future energy requirements and intelligently decides whether renewable energy should be **used, stored, or prioritized for high-demand loads**.

### 🎯 Core Idea

> **Monitor → Predict → Optimize → Control**

Instead of only monitoring current energy consumption, GreenGrid AI predicts **where and when energy will be needed** and proactively manages available renewable energy.

For example, when students leave hostels for classes around 8 AM, hostel demand decreases while academic-building demand increases. GreenGrid AI predicts this shift and optimizes renewable-energy utilization and storage to reduce unnecessary grid dependency and energy wastage.
